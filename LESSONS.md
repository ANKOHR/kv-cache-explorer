# Lessons for KOML

Logical KV payload grows linearly with tokens and KV heads. A strict budget boundary is easy to audit with exact integers, but payload is not process RAM. Supplied timings cannot establish hardware behavior without a recorded measurement protocol and comparable baselines.

The validated assumption is that explicit inputs permit exact deterministic accounting: four planted groups reconcile to 32000 hypothetical bytes and 100 budget violations. Mutating a fixture changes results, demonstrating calculation rather than hardcoding. Synthetic evidence does not validate real-world performance or adoption.

Operating rule: separate analytical quantities from observations in field names and provenance, and require explicit comparable baselines before presenting any performance delta. Keep ground truth independent of the engine.

Verdict: pending fourteen days of real-use evidence, as specified in README. No final adoption verdict is invented at build time.
